# Study 3 — HER activity of 238 Fe–Co–Ni–Pd–Pt compositions

Xiangyi Shan, Professor Jiang, and Professor Zhou shared the experimental hydrogen evolution reaction (HER) data for 238 Fe–Co–Ni–Pd–Pt alloy compositions from their Study 3 publication. We use the relative current measured at −0.15 V as the experimental HER activity for each composition.

[exp_activity_238.json](./exp_activity_238.json) contains one record per composition. The `fractions` field gives the atomic fraction of each element (Fe, Co, Ni, Pd, and Pt). The `j_relative_m0p15V` field gives the HER activity and corresponds to the spreadsheet column `j_relative (-0.15 V)`.

## Reference

Shan, X. *et al.* Accelerating the Discovery of Efficient High-Entropy Alloy Electrocatalysts: High-Throughput Experimentation and Data-Driven Strategies. *Nano Lett.* **24**, 11632–11640 (2024).
